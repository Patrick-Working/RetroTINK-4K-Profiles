Kenbakes' Neo-Retro CRT Prescaling Profiles

Here are some Switch Profiles that pre-scales on the back of @Kuro Houou's  FV310 CRT Look for the following Use Cases to have sick crispy scanlines with "Neo-Retro Games", using @Try4ce's pre-scaling methods from My Life in Gaming's RT4K video.

Thanks Try! Thanks Kurou!

From a Base 1080p Resolution

     Neo-Retro - 1080p to 270p FV310
          Applicable Games
               - Hyper Light Drifter
               - TMNT Shredders Revenge
          Instructions
               -Just Load the Profile and have fun!

     Neo-Retro - 1080p to 270p [Tengo Aspect] FV310
          Notes
               -With Integer Interpolation Scaling for Use with Tengo Project Games
          Applicable Games 
               - Pocky & Rocky Reshrined
               - Wild Guns Reloaded
          Instructions
               - Go into the Settings menu for tengo project games to set the scaling to 100 for square pixels, apply the profile and have fun!
