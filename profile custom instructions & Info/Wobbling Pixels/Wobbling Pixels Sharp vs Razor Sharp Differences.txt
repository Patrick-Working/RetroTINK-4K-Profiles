---------------
Sharp Profiles: 
---------------

+ Provide display aspect ratio as you would see on a correct calibrated CRT TV
- A little loss in image sharpness compared to razor sharp profiles

---------------------
Razor Sharp Profiles:
---------------------

Integer scale and/or square pixels (Pixel Aspect Ratio (PAR))
Square Pixels profiles can be the better choice with some games that are designed 
with square pixels in mind e.g. ovals will look like circles and rectangles like squares.  

+ Provide a bit sharper image
- Aspect ratio different to sharp profiles
