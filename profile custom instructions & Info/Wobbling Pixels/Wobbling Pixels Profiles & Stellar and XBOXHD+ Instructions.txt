- XBOX installed with Stellar or XBOXHD+ hdmi mod
- primary for 480p but also works with 720p, 1080i Xbox games
- 4K (3840 x 2160) output resolution

---------------------------
Stellar & XBOXHD+ Settings
---------------------------

Access the Stellar & XBOXHD+ settings

1. Go to System Settings and make sure only 480p is enabled. 

2. Go to "Video Settings" and set:

Target Resolution: 480p
4:3 Widescreen: Never
Pre-Scale 480p Correction: OFF (disable it)

3. Go to "Advanced Settings" and set "HDMI Colorspace" to "RGB"

4. Press B to save and exit. 

-------------------
How to use profiles
-------------------

"Xbox 720x - Sharp" - For games that are rendered and output at 720x480p
"Xbox 640x to 720x - Sharp" - For games that are scaled by Stellar or XBOXHD+ to render resolution 
                              of 640x480p in a 720x480p frame with PAR 10/11
"Xbox PAR 640x - Sharp" - For games that are rendered and output at 640x480p with PAR 1:1

1. Launch your Xbox game. 
2. Access the Retrotink 4K menu.
3. Load the profile "Xbox 720x - Sharp"
4. Go to "Advanced Settings" -> "Scaling/Cropping" on the Retrotink 4k
5. If you don't see black bars (pillarboxes) on the sides of the image between the blue cropping area and the
   game image keep the profile "Xbox 720x - Sharp" and have fun. :)
6. If you see black bars (pillarboxes) on the sides of the image between the blue cropping area and the
   game image then load one of the two profiles:

"Xbox PAR 640x - Sharp
"Xbox 640x to 720x - Sharp"

You can decide which one is more suitable for the game and you.
Most 640x480 Xbox games should be better suitable with "Xbox PAR 640x - Sharp" profile.
