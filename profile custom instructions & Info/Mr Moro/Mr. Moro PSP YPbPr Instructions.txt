Before you start playing your game
1) Go to settings on the left of the main PSP screen
2) Go to "Connected Display Settings" and press X
3) Go to "Flicker Reduction" and select "Off"
4) Go to "Component/D-Terminal Ouput"
4a) If you are playing PSP content, select Progressive
4b) If you are playing PS1 Content, select Interlace
5) Hold the "display" button on the PSP until your display comes up on your screen
6) select the content you want to play
7) select the profile you want to use
