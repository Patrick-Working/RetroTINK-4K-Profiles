Warning: These profiles are saved with 4K60 output, if you don't have a 4K display and you will lose signal hit the 1080P or 720P button on the remote
to switch resolutions to one supported by your display.

The goal of these profiles is to provide very accurate of Emulation of actual CRT TV's and PVMs as possible (I own each of the TV's/PVMs for which a profile was built) with, optimized Scanlines and Masks that can be used with any combination of Scaling profiles or inputs.

These profiles have been optimized for proper Video Levels as meassured by HCFR and a Calibrite Display Plus Colorimeter.
Details on the analysis done and how the optimized profiles can be found in the paper located here, https://tinyurl.com/tink4k

Profiles were created on an LG C1, although they should work similar across other makes and models, TV's with a RGB Pixel structure could have the mask strength
increased by 1 or 2 levels and possibly look even better but going to -1 or 0 may severly impact color accuracy so be careful.  

Follow best practices for inital setup of your TV settings, don't use Vivid modes, turn off all forms
of processing, etc.. normal procedures to ensure proper display of content.  

Last, all profiles are made for maximum compatibility, so it is suggested to turn on things like GenLock or 10bit color if you display supports it.

***************************************************************************************************************************************************************

SDR

Profiles for SDR should work well across all makes and models. In order to reach proper brigthness levels after enabling the scanlines and masks, please modify
the following settings on your TV. 

1. In your TV settings, set Contrast to 100% or Max
2. Set Panel Pixel Brightness to 100% or Max (As found on the LG OLEDs or maybe under a similar name on other OLEDs), this is
not screen brightness as that washes out blacks.


***************************************************************************************************************************************************************

HDR and HDR with BFI

1. Enable a Cinema or Game Optimized profile and disable any extra processing
2. Turn off Tone Mapping (MUST DO!!!)
3. Ensure Panel Pixel Brightness and Contrast at at 100%, Normal brightness should be in the middle or 50%.
